How to install

Windows

  1. Download the Windows ZIP.
  2. Right-click it and choose Extract All.
  3. Open the extracted folder and double-click TheJumbieGambit.exe.

Blue “Windows protected your PC” box? Click More info, then Run anyway. It appears because this is an early test build.

Mac

  1. Download the Mac ZIP. Your Mac unzips it into The Jumbie Gambit.app.
  2. Drag the app into your Applications folder.
  3. Control-click the app, choose Open, then Open again.

Mac says it can’t open the app? Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only need to do this once.
